# Annex C: Bibliography (Informative)

This bibliography identifies background material useful for designing, applying, and evaluating TimeCard implementations. These documents are informative. Their implementation is not required for conformance unless a normative clause separately cites a document listed in Clause 2.

## C.1 Standards and Industry Profiles

- **[B1]** CENELEC EN 55032:2015+A11:2020 and EN 55035:2017+A11:2020, *Electromagnetic compatibility of multimedia equipment--Emission Requirements* and *Immunity Requirements*.
- **[B2]** ETSI EN 300 019 series, *Environmental Engineering (EE); Environmental conditions and environmental tests for telecommunications equipment*.
- **[B3]** IEC 60068-2-6:2007, *Environmental testing--Part 2-6: Tests--Test Fc: Vibration (sinusoidal)*. [<https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/544>]
- **[B4]** IEC 60068-2-27:2008, *Environmental testing--Part 2-27: Tests--Test Ea and guidance: Shock*. [<https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/514>]
- **[B5]** IEC 61000-4-2:2025, *Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC)--Part 4-2: Testing and measurement techniques--Electrostatic discharge immunity test*. [<https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/68954>]
- **[B6]** IEC 62368-1:2023, *Audio/video, information and communication technology equipment--Part 1: Safety requirements*. [<https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/69308>]
- **[B7]** IEEE Std C37.238-2017, *IEEE Standard Profile for Use of IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol in Power System Applications*, including IEEE Std C37.238a-2023. [<https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/C37.238/6650/>]
- **[B8]** ISO/IEC 17025:2017, *General requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories*.
- **[B9]** ITU-T Recommendation G.8275.1/Y.1369.1 (02/2026), *Precision time protocol telecom profile for phase/time synchronization with full timing support from the network*. [<https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8275.1-202602-P/en>]
- **[B10]** ITU-T Recommendation G.8275.2/Y.1369.2 (02/2026), *Precision time protocol telecom profile for phase/time synchronization with partial timing support from the network*. [<https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8275.2-202602-P/en>]
- **[B11]** ITU-T Recommendation G.8271/Y.1366 (03/2020), *Time and phase synchronization aspects of telecommunication networks*, including Amendment 1 (08/2024). [<https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-G.8271/en>]
