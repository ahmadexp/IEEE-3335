# Rodney Cummings: P3335 v8 comment dispositions

Editorial responses for reviewer and working group (WG) review, 2026-11-10. These are proposed dispositions, not votes, reviewer acceptance, or a finding of ballot readiness.

Review copies: [revised unofficial draft](../IEEE3335.pdf) and [response workbook with original comments and WG decision list](../outputs/rodney-feedback-20261110/P3335v8_Rodney_Cummings_Proposed_Responses.xlsx).

Source: Rodney Cummings (Keysight), `P3335v8_Comments_RodneyC_Nov_2026.xlsx`, worksheet `Comments`, rows 6 through 24. All 19 comments are marked Technical. The page, subclause, and line below refer to **v8**, not the repaginated revision. "Must" reproduces the reviewer's "Must Be Satisfied" flag. **Applied** means the concern is addressed in the unofficial draft, sometimes with alternative wording. **Partial** means a change was made but a requested decision or wider review remains open.

**Summary:** 13 applied, 6 partial. All five Must items have a proposed change for Rodney to review: RC-04, RC-05, RC-06, RC-10, and RC-15.

## Decisions for the WG

| Item | Question for decision | Current text and next step |
|------|-----------------------|----------------------------|
| R1 (RC-02) | Should the baseline control model be normative, informative, or paired with a common optional binding? | The draft retains required semantics. Compare two independent bindings against the same read/status tests; decide separately whether transport-level interchange is a goal. Coordinate with AI-006 and AI-018. |
| R2 (RC-03) | Which fields must be public, supplied with the product, or available only under controlled access? | Keep 4.7 pending decision. Ask suppliers and evaluators to mark a sample datasheet with the minimum fields they can supply and independently verify. Decide jointly with Kevin's WG-K08. |
| R3 (RC-13) | Keep the reporting-only base, reduce the mandatory metric set, or rely entirely on external profiles? | Keep 6.1 pending decision. Compare a product with a named external profile and one without; identify which declarations can be removed without losing comparability. |
| R4 (RC-18) | Should G.703 19.2 or its Annex B option become the baseline for every physical 1PPS output? | Keep existing P3335 limits. Compare thresholds, waveform, cable and termination, compatibility, and test cost with WG-K02. Decide independently whether to specify an optional serial ToD profile. |
| R5 (RC-19) | Remove ADEV entirely, move all mention to an informative annex, or keep a conditional reporting rule? | No ADEV result is required by base conformance. Ask frequency-output implementers which claimed profiles use it and which evidence is needed before deciding. |

**Before closing the review:** Clause owners should use the [requirements index](../REQUIREMENTS_INDEX.md) to identify the subject, trigger, observable result, and verification method for each remaining mandatory provision (RC-01). Return the revised PDF and these proposed dispositions to Rodney, particularly for the five Must items. Record his response and WG decisions separately.
