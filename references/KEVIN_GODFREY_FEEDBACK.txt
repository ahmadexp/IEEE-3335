KEVIN GODFREY FEEDBACK SUMMARY
IEEE P3335 unofficial proposal
Revision date: 3 November 2026

51 review items: 34 applied, 6 partially addressed, and 11 deferred.

Applied means incorporated into the unofficial draft, not approved by the
Working Group (WG) or accepted by the reviewer.

APPLIED (34)

KG-04: Added an informative G.703 measurement-interface summary and source
link, without changing the existing 1PPS requirements.

KG-08: Clarified that backup energy is optional and distinguished retained
configuration, RTC operation, timescale maintenance, and actual timing service.

KG-09: Added battery chemistry, ratings, maintenance, disposal, and applicable
transport-documentation requirements.

KG-13: Defined gateware and distinguished it from processor-executed instructions.

KG-14: Removed unused abbreviations and unit entries; added BNC.

KG-15: Explained BeiDou and Galileo as constellation names, not acronyms
requiring expansion.

KG-18: Permitted precise references to external performance specifications
while retaining uncovered P3335 declarations.

KG-20: Clarified that UTC(k) identifies a reference timescale, not a transport
interface.

KG-21: Removed undefined environmental-profile examples while preserving
mandatory environmental declarations.

KG-23: Required appropriate functional and performance test evidence;
clarified supplier evaluation and representative-configuration testing.

KG-24: Updated UTC-conversion guidance to distinguish historical conversions,
valid offsets, and future UTC policy.

KG-25: Replaced ambiguous pulse-jitter wording with time error and timing
variation, including sampling and bandwidth limitations.

KG-26: Distinguished binding numeric requirements from informative references
and separately claimed performance classes.

KG-27: Simplified the performance-category list; retained detailed metrics in
Clause 6 and Annex A.

KG-28: Permitted documented local status indicators without mandating LEDs or
replacing control-interface status.

KG-29: Required externally observable disciplining behavior instead of
disclosure of proprietary algorithms or loop topology.

KG-30: Clarified laboratory, GNSS, and simulator references, traceability,
absolute versus relative measurements, and correlated errors.

KG-31: Allowed appropriate time-domain measurements while preserving
safeguards against unsupported spectral-mask claims.

KG-32: Retained ADEV and clarified its distinction from TDEV and frequency offset.

KG-33: Clarified holdover-profile references and separated absolute or
initial-offset-relative error bounds from MTIE.

KG-34: Allowed referenced transition requirements while retaining uncovered
host and driver lifecycle cases.

KG-35: Required precise identification of specification editions, clauses,
classes, masks, conditions, and acceptance rules.

KG-36: Required G.703 compatibility claims to identify the specific interface
and applicable provisions.

KG-37: Added firmware, gateware, software updates, and restart to
output-behavior declarations.

KG-38: Explicitly included 50 ohm BNC connectors while retaining TNC and the
existing connector requirements.

KG-43: Specified environmental units, permitted prefixes, pressure references,
and supplementary unit conversions.

KG-44: Added relevant references to each application subsection.

KG-45: Clarified identification of performance masks, parameters, observation
intervals, filtering, and acceptance rules.

KG-46: Specified seconds or clearly labelled submultiples for TDEV and MTIE.

KG-47: Reworked Annex A.6 around pulse and event timing variation.

KG-48: Made the test fixture interface-neutral and added the instrument's
measurement-reference connection.

KG-49: Separated reference origin and timescale from the interface carrying
that reference.

KG-50: Replaced the diagram's fixed analyzer-metric list with an
applicable-metrics label; retained ADEV elsewhere.

KG-51: Added conditional PTP, IRIG, and other host/logical output test examples
alongside PTM.

Pending: Adoption as a binding P3335 profile limit.

KG-12: Added the public G.Sup92 catalog reference.

KG-22: Explicitly accommodated external performance classes.
Pending: Identification and review of the unfinished G.FTS-Lite-clk specification.

DEFERRED (11)

KG-03: Adopting G.703 as the normative 1PPS baseline requires WG agreement.

KG-05: Changing pulse width, rise time, and timing marker depends on the same
baseline decision.

KG-07: New receive-cable delay-compensation objects require agreed units,
sign, range, persistence, and control semantics.

KG-10: Mandatory battery telemetry and sensing require agreed applicability
and measurement requirements.

KG-11: Updating the normative G.8260 reference to 08/2026 requires authorized
access and technical review.

KG-16: Mandatory independent timing-verification access requires agreement on
acceptable verification routes.

KG-19: Mandatory physical test points for embedded implementations require a
physical-versus-logical access decision.

KG-39: Mandatory equipment serial numbers and exterior labels require scope
and identity rules.

KG-40: Mandatory power monitoring and alarms require detection, threshold,
and reporting specifications.

KG-41: Battery-health monitoring is consolidated with KG-10.

KG-42: GNSS and other input-delay correction objects are consolidated with KG-07.
