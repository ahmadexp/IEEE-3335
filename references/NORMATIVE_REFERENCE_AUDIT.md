## Result

Every Clause 2 entry is cited in normative text and has an identified implementation role. No reference is retained solely for background. This technical-use result does not establish normative-reference eligibility. The IPMI source requires specific IEEE SA review of its embedded terms, in addition to edition ratification and the general editorial/legal review.

## IPMI source correction, 17 September 2026

The [DMTF publication catalog](https://www.dmtf.org/dsp/DSP0236) identifies DSP0236 as the Management Component Transport Protocol (MCTP) Base Specification. It is not the IPMI specification.

[Intel's IPMI project page](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/products/docs/servers/ipmi/ipmi-home.html) links to the [IPMI v2.0, Revision 1.1, E7 document](https://www.intel.com/content/dam/www/public/us/en/documents/specification-updates/ipmi-intelligent-platform-mgt-interface-spec-2nd-gen-v2-0-spec-update.pdf). The cover and revision history identify 1 October 2013 and the E7 markup dated 21 April 2015. Printed page 3 includes NDA and separate adopter-agreement language. The document identity has been corrected in Clauses 2 and 8; eligibility remains open under AI-007 and AI-030.

[IEEE SA Operations Manual 6.4.6](https://standards.ieee.org/about/policies/opman/sect6/) addresses normative-reference availability and commercial terms. The WG Chair and IEEE SA project editor need to determine an acceptable reference disposition; this audit does not interpret the enforceability of the source's terms.
